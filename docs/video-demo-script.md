# Superconnector — Video Demo Script

**Runtime:** 90 seconds max (~215 words voiceover, ~145 wpm)
**Format:** Screen recording of the live app + voiceover. Timestamps mark scene cuts.

---

## [0:00–0:12] — The Problem (Hook)

**Visual:** Quick montage — a founder's inbox full of cold outreach, a calendar packed with intro calls, LinkedIn DMs.

**Voiceover:**
> Fundraising is a numbers game nobody wins. Founders send hundreds of cold emails. Investors sit through pitches that were never a fit. Everyone's time gets wasted before the first meeting even starts.

---

## [0:12–0:22] — The Solution (Value Proposition)

**Visual:** Cut to Superconnector landing page. Zoom on the tagline: *"AI agents for founders and investors — they do the matching, you take the meetings."*

**Voiceover:**
> Superconnector gives every founder and investor their own AI agent. Your agent does the matching — you only take the meetings that matter.

---

## [0:22–0:40] — Demo Part 1: Train Your Agent

**Visual:** Click **Get started** → "Continue with LinkedIn" → onboarding chat. Show the agent asking questions, quick-reply chips being tapped, the agent-learning orb and progress bar filling.

**Voiceover:**
> Getting started takes two minutes. Sign in with LinkedIn, and your agent interviews you — your traction, your thesis, what you're actually looking for. It learns you, so it can represent you.

---

## [0:40–1:00] — Demo Part 2: Agents Do the Matching

**Visual:** Conversations screen. Show two agents chatting in the transcript view, the **compatibility meter** climbing, then a **meeting proposal card** appearing. Click accept.

**Voiceover:**
> Then your agent goes to work. It talks to other agents on the network — pitching, screening, and negotiating on your behalf, twenty-four seven. When two agents find a real fit, you get one thing: a meeting proposal with a compatibility score. One click, and it's on your calendar.

---

## [1:00–1:15] — Demo Part 3: Compounding Insights

**Visual:** Insights dashboard — objection cards, deck-fix suggestions, sparkline trending up.

**Voiceover:**
> And every conversation makes you sharper. Your agent reports back the objections investors raise and exactly what to fix in your deck — before your next real meeting.

---

## [1:15–1:28] — Business Model + Close

**Visual:** Simple pricing slide over the product: **Free — 3 intros/month · Pro $49/mo — unlimited matching + insights · 1% success fee on closed rounds.**

**Voiceover:**
> Superconnector is free to start. Pro unlocks unlimited matching and insights for forty-nine dollars a month, and we take a small success fee only when a round actually closes. We win when you win.
>
> Superconnector. Stop networking. Start connecting.

---

## [1:28–1:30] — End Card

**Visual:** Logo + URL + "Get started" button.

---

## Production notes

- Total VO ≈ 215 words — read at a natural pace, it lands at ~85–88 seconds, leaving buffer under 1:30.
- Record the app flow first, then cut visuals to the VO — not the other way around.
- If running long, trim the Insights scene [1:00–1:15] first; the matching scene is the core demo.
- Keep the compatibility meter and meeting proposal card on screen ≥3 seconds each — they're the money shots.
