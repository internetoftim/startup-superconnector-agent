# 90-Second Video Script v6 — Superconnector (three-role marketplace)

Hero = founder-agent -> [matchmaker] -> investor-agent, brokered. Read as a HUB
with two spokes, never a three-way shout. Opens on the real interview quote.
~220 words; if hot, cut the tool name-drop, never the hero.
(Get the organiser's OK for the quote, or anonymise: "an SSC I interviewed.")

---

## [0:00-0:12] - The evidence (12s) - quote card + VO
[Quote card:]
"I was using my time replying to the same people every day... not getting new
people." - startup super-connector, interviewed the night before
[VO:] "Founders want the right investors. Investors want curated dealflow. The
connectors in the middle drown in DMs making it happen."

## [0:12-0:24] - Insight + founder fit (12s) - screen: three phones, one hub
[Founder chats / investor chats / the connector's overloaded inbox]
"Founders and investors are getting personal agents. But their agents can't just
cold-call each other - no trust, no curation, no privacy. Someone has to broker.
I've run 30+ events - that someone is me. So I built the matchmaker as an agent."

## [0:24-1:09] - BEAT A - the hero (45s) - screen: hub + two spokes
[Three panes: LEFT founder-agent, CENTER matchmaker, RIGHT investor-agent.]
"Left: a founder's agent, raising a seed round. Right: an investor's agent.
Center: my matchmaker agent - and the two never talk directly. Watch it broker."
[Founder-agent -> matchmaker: robotics seed, wants a deep-tech investor, in
person Sept; shares public one-liner + '3 paid pilots' only]
[Matchmaker curates -> matches Aya, thesis-fit, opted-in -> offers to investor-
agent -> investor's filter PASSes]
"Now the trust work only the middle can do. The investor's agent asks for the
deck - the matchmaker WITHHOLDS it, because the founder consented to public info
only, and escalates the request to the founder. It won't hand the founder the
investor's private calendar - just the one mutual slot. And when the founder's
agent asks 'will she actually invest?' - it refuses to vouch on that. It vouches
on FIT, never on outcome, and escalates to me."
[Escalation lines print; tri-party agreement prints]
"Three agents, one signed agreement - who's introduced, on what consented info,
when they meet. A week of DMs, brokered in one exchange."

## [1:09-1:19] - BEAT B - coda (10s, only if built) - screen: logistics
[Matchmaker, given the agreement:] "Then it just executes - calendar hold,
drafts the intro, reserves the summit seat."

## [1:19-1:30] - Why now / why me (11s) - talking head
"A2A hit v1.0 this year - everyone's getting an agent, which is exactly why you
need a trusted one in the middle. Founders, investors, connectors - same engine,
different profile. I'm an SSC, I contribute to AG2, I built my own matchmaker.
Solo, five hours, on GStack."

[End card: Superconnector - trusted matchmaking, agent to agent - Tim Santos]

---

## Rules
- Hero must READ hub-and-spoke: matchmaker in the CENTER doing what neither side
  can. The dual-consent (deck withheld / calendar protected) + the vouch REFUSAL
  are the trust proof - protect them; they are the whole defensibility story.
- Keep 3 panes max. If it looks like three agents shouting, simplify the render.
- Beat B is cuttable; if unbuilt, skip 1:09-1:19, give seconds to why-now.
- Claims discipline: "interviewed an SSC" = true, NOT "validated." "A2A-inspired
  demo; production rides A2A v1.0." Founder facts CV-verifiable.
- Legibility > polish: zoom the three panes + the agreement for phone screens.
