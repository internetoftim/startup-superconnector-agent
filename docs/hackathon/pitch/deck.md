# Deck — Superconnector (6 slides, YC-judged build competition)

## Slide 1 — Title
**Superconnector** — your network, represented.
One agent that speaks for you, agent-to-agent. Built solo on GStack · Tim Santos

## Slide 2 — The problem (the quote IS the slide)
"I was using my time replying to the same people every day… not getting new
people." — startup super-connector, interviewed the night before this hackathon.
A connector's value is their network; their bottleneck is their own attention,
fragmented across five apps.

## Slide 3 — The insight
**As founders and investors get their own agents, those agents still can't
cold-call each other — no trust, no curation, no privacy.** Someone must broker,
and it's the connector both sides already trust. A2A v1.0 (Linux Foundation,
150+ orgs, native in the major clouds) makes agent-to-agent dealflow possible;
the trusted matchmaker in the MIDDLE makes it safe.

## Slide 4 — The product (screenshot: hub + two spokes + tri-party agreement)
Three roles — founder, investor, matchmaker — run ONE proxy engine over ONE
profile schema (identity + mandate + offer/seek + consent). Founders' and
investors' agents negotiate THROUGH the matchmaker, which curates fit, enforces
BOTH sides' consent (deck withheld, availability protected), and vouches only on
fit — escalating outcome questions. Every deal is a signed tri-party agreement.
A new role is a new profile, not new code.

## Slide 5 — Why now / why me
A2A standardised cross-org agent traffic this year. I'm a super-connector —
Angel Squad, VC scout, AI Hive, 30+ events across four cities — and I contribute
to AG2, the multi-agent framework. I built my own representative and interviewed
a fellow connector the night before; heard my own pain back.

## Slide 6 — The company
**Land the matchmakers → own the trusted layer between founders and investors.**
The matchmaker's curated network + reputation compounds with every brokered deal.
Intros today; then advisory, hiring, event access, secondary dealflow — any
two-sided market where a trusted broker beats a raw directory. Same engine, more
profiles.
